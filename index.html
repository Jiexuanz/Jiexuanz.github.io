Jiexuan Zhang 张杰宣

M.Sc. Student · School of Computer Science · Peking University

I am a Master student at Peking University, advised by Prof. Jian Zhang of the VILLA Lab. Prior to that, I received my B.Eng. in 2024 from Beihang University, Shenyuan Honor College, majoring in Software Engineering.

Research Interests

  • Image & Video Generation. Building generative models for high-quality visual content, with emphasis on controllability and consistency across outputs.
  • Diffusion Models. Exploring training, alignment, and post-training methods for diffusion-based generative systems.
  • Post-Training & Evaluation. Developing reward modeling and quality assessment techniques to improve generative model performance.
